# Category Description
### All three categories serve a specific purpose. (Sketches, CAD Progress and Renders)

- ## Sketches
  The "Sketches" category contains all the images showing how the pre-prototype was initially visualized on paper. Every concept was sketched by hand during class, without the aid of a computer, and served as the foundation for the 3D CAD model.

- ## CAD Progress
  The "CAD Progress" category is self-explanatory; it features screenshots documenting the entire 10-day CAD development sprint.

- ## Renders
  The "Renders" category showcases the CAD model in a high-quality visual format, featuring simple animations of its deployment, autonomous calibration, and other operations.
